## The Company Behind the Platform

ChangeNOW operates as ChangeNOW OU, a private limited company registered in Estonia. Estonia's incorporation and financial regulatory environment has been the domicile of choice for a number of legitimate European crypto businesses, in part because Estonia was among the first EU member states to establish a clear licensing framework for virtual asset service providers (VASPs). The Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) oversees VASP licensing; licensed entities are subject to AML, KYC, and transaction monitoring obligations under EU directives.

ChangeNOW has operated continuously from this base since 2017 — nine years as of 2026. Longevity is not proof of legitimacy on its own, but it is meaningful evidence in a market where exit scams typically materialize within the first two years of operation. No documented exit scam event, rug pull, or sudden platform shutdown has been attributed to ChangeNOW in its operational history.

## Understanding Compliance Holds: Scam Signal or AML Working?

The compliance hold is the source of most "is this a scam?" anxiety, and it warrants direct examination. When ChangeNOW pauses a transaction pending identity verification, the user-facing experience is alarming: funds have been sent, the transaction is not completing, and communication from the platform may feel opaque.

What is actually happening is the application of AML compliance procedures required by ChangeNOW's regulatory environment. Estonia's FIU-regulated VASPs are obligated to apply transaction monitoring, screen counterparties against sanctions lists, and conduct enhanced due diligence on transactions that exceed risk thresholds by amount, jurisdiction, or counterparty profile. A compliance hold is the operational expression of that obligation.

The distinguishing characteristic between a compliance hold at a legitimate platform and a fund withholding at a scam platform is resolution. At legitimate platforms, compliance holds resolve — through identity verification, source of funds documentation, or elapsed review periods — and funds are either completed or returned. At scam platforms, funds are not returned regardless of documentation provided. ChangeNOW's review record, including Roman's critical case, does not document a pattern of permanent fund seizure after compliance documentation is provided. The process is frustrating; it is not a theft.

Jonathan B's experience illustrates the support dimension from a different angle. His ATOM transaction encountered a memo error — a technical failure mode, not a compliance hold — and ChangeNOW support resolved it within four hours. Swift resolution of a technical error is not a characteristic of platforms operating in bad faith.

## The Regulatory Environment: What It Means in Practice

ChangeNOW is not a centralized exchange operating under a MiCA license or a U.S. Money Services Business registration. It operates in the VASP framework applicable to Estonian crypto businesses under EU AML directives. This means it has AML obligations, is subject to FIU oversight, and must maintain compliance programs — but it is not regulated in the same sense as a bank or a licensed securities firm.

For users, this has practical implications. Standard swaps under risk thresholds proceed without identity verification. Larger transactions, or those flagging counterparty screening, may trigger the compliance process described above. The platform does not operate with zero regulatory accountability, as some characterizations of non-KYC exchanges imply, but it also does not offer the consumer protection infrastructure of a regulated financial institution.

This is the honest middle ground: a regulated VASP operating with genuine compliance obligations, offering a user experience that is largely frictionless for the majority of users and occasionally burdensome for those whose transactions trigger review.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is ChangeNOW a scam?**
No. The available evidence — company registration, nine-year operational history, 450,000+ Trustpilot reviews, no documented exit scam, no disclosed security breach — does not support a scam classification. Compliance holds, which generate most "scam" concerns, are the documented mechanism of ChangeNOW's AML compliance program and are distinct from fund theft.

**Is ChangeNOW regulated?**
ChangeNOW operates as ChangeNOW OU under Estonia's VASP regulatory framework, supervised by the Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit. This subjects the company to EU AML and KYC directives. It is not regulated as a bank or licensed securities firm and does not offer equivalent consumer protections.

**What causes a compliance hold on ChangeNOW?**
Compliance holds are triggered by transaction monitoring systems evaluating amount thresholds, counterparty screening against sanctions lists, geographic risk factors, and behavioral patterns. Larger transactions are more likely to trigger review. When a hold occurs, ChangeNOW typically requests identity verification and, in some cases, source of funds documentation via email.

**Is my money safe on ChangeNOW?**
ChangeNOW is a non-custodial swap platform — it does not hold user funds in accounts. Funds are in transit during a swap; in the event of a compliance hold, they are held pending resolution, not seized permanently. No exchange or swap platform of any type is entirely risk-free. For standard swap amounts on common pairs, the risk profile is comparable to other established swap providers in the same market segment.

**Has ChangeNOW ever been hacked?**
No publicly disclosed security breach resulting in user fund loss has been attributed to ChangeNOW in its nine-year operational history. This does not guarantee future security, but it distinguishes ChangeNOW from competitors that have experienced documented incidents.
