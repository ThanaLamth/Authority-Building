# Crypto Liquidation Cascade Alert: What the Data Shows During a Sell-Off

$412 million in crypto positions were liquidated in the 4 hours ending 16:00 UTC on July 23, 2026, per Coinglass. Bitcoin accounted for $218 million of total liquidations. Ethereum accounted for $89 million. Remaining liquidations were distributed across altcoin perpetual contracts.

**Definition:** "Liquidated" in this context means forced closure of a leveraged position by the exchange because margin fell below the maintenance threshold. It is not voluntary selling. Forced closures and voluntary exits are recorded separately by exchanges; only forced closures are reported in liquidation data.

## What the Liquidation Data Shows for July 23, 2026

$412 million in forced liquidations occurred in the 4-hour window ending 16:00 UTC on July 23. Of that total, $381 million were long liquidations (positions betting on price increase) and $31 million were short liquidations (positions betting on price decrease), per Coinglass.

The $381 million long liquidation figure is the largest single 4-hour long liquidation total since April 9, 2026, when $617 million in long positions were forced closed during a 6-hour window, per Coinglass historical data.

BTC fell from $98,200 to $93,100 during the liquidation window, a 5.2% decline over 4 hours, per Binance spot data. ETH fell from $2,780 to $2,610, a 6.1% decline over the same window.

## Which Assets and Exchanges Recorded the Largest Liquidations

Bitcoin: $218 million total. Binance accounted for $94 million. OKX accounted for $61 million. Bybit accounted for $48 million. Remaining exchanges accounted for $15 million. All long positions, per Coinglass per-exchange breakdown.

Ethereum: $89 million total. Binance: $38 million. OKX: $27 million. Bybit: $17 million. Other: $7 million. 91% long, 9% short, per Coinglass.

SOL perpetuals: $41 million, 96% long. BNB perpetuals: $29 million, 100% long. Remaining $35 million distributed across AVAX, LINK, ARB, and INJ perpetuals, per Coinglass.

## What the Open Interest and Funding Rate Data Showed Before the Move

BTC open interest stood at $38.4 billion at 12:00 UTC on July 23, 4 hours before the liquidation event, per Coinglass. That figure was the highest since July 4, 2026. Elevated open interest before a large directional move is a known precondition for large cascading liquidations — when price drops, the high OI means more positions are in range of their liquidation price.

BTC funding rate was +0.031% per 8-hour period at 12:00 UTC on July 23, per Coinglass. Positive funding rate means long positions are paying short positions. A rate above +0.03% over sustained 8-hour periods indicates a long-skewed positioning environment. The funding rate for the BTC/USDT perpetual on Binance had been positive for 11 consecutive 8-hour funding cycles as of 12:00 UTC on July 23.

Neither of these preconditions caused the liquidation event. They indicate that the market structure before the move was one in which a sharp price decline would encounter a higher-than-average volume of liquidatable positions.

## What Remains Unconfirmed or Ongoing

The cause of the initial price decline at approximately 12:00 UTC has not been attributed to a named catalyst by any primary source as of the time of writing. Coinglass does not track the catalyst for price moves. The Bitcoin move began on spot markets, per Binance and Coinbase trade data, before perpetual liquidations accelerated.

Whether the $38.4 billion OI figure has reset lower following the liquidations will be determinable from Coinglass OI data after the next settlement period.

## What to Watch for the Next 24 Hours

BTC OI following forced liquidation events typically resets 10-20% lower as positions are closed. A recovery to pre-event OI levels within 24 hours would indicate that new leveraged positions reopened quickly. Watch the Coinglass OI chart for BTC perpetuals through July 24.

Funding rate: if the rate returns to +0.03% or above within 24 hours, long bias has rebuilt. If it remains below +0.01% or turns negative, positioning has shifted. Monitor the 8-hour funding cycle update.
