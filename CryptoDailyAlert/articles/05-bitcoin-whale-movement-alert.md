# Bitcoin Whale Movement Alert: On-Chain Signals and Exchange Transfer Data

1,840 BTC moved from a wallet labeled "Unknown — Active since 2019" to a Binance deposit address at 07:14 UTC on July 25, 2026, per Arkham Intelligence data. At BTC's price of $96,400 at time of transfer, the transaction was valued at approximately $177 million.

*"Whale" in this alert refers to a single on-chain transaction above 1,000 BTC. This threshold is applied consistently across all whale movement alerts on this site.*

## What the On-Chain Data Shows

Transaction hash: confirmed on-chain at block 858,341 at 07:14 UTC, per mempool.space. Transfer amount: 1,840 BTC in a single UTXO output. Fee paid: 0.00041 BTC. Transaction size: 1 input, 2 outputs — the second output returned 12 BTC to the originating cluster, per Arkham Intelligence.

The sending address cluster has not transferred funds to an exchange in the prior 180 days, per Arkham Intelligence historical data. The 30-day average for transactions above 1,000 BTC is 2.1 transactions per day, per Glassnode data for the period June 25–July 25, 2026.

## Which Wallets or Addresses Are Involved

The sending address is labeled "Unknown — Active since 2019" by Arkham Intelligence. No verified entity label (exchange, fund, OTC desk, custodian) is attached to the source wallet. The receiving address is labeled as a Binance deposit address by both Arkham Intelligence and Glassnode.

"Labeled as a Binance deposit address" means the receiving address has a documented history of routing funds that subsequently appeared in Binance's exchange balance cluster, per on-chain analysis methodology. It does not mean Binance has confirmed the owner of the sending wallet.

## What the Transfer Direction and Destination Indicates

1,840 BTC moved to a Binance deposit address. This is an observable on-chain fact. It is not the same as 1,840 BTC being sold.

A transfer to an exchange deposit address means the funds are now addressable within the exchange's internal accounting. Whether the holder intends to sell, trade against derivatives, use as collateral, or transfer to a second wallet within the exchange cannot be determined from on-chain data alone.
