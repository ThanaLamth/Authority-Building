# Ethereum Staking Withdrawal Alert: Queue Length and Exit Data

Ethereum validator exit queue reached 4,812 validators on July 25, 2026, at 14:00 UTC, per beaconcha.in. Estimated wait time for full exit is 4.3 days at the current churn rate of 1,800 validators per day.

## What the Current Exit Queue Shows

4,812 validators are in the exit queue as of 14:00 UTC on July 25, 2026. Each validator holds 32 ETH. Total ETH pending withdrawal is approximately 153,984 ETH, or $408 million at the July 25 ETH price of $2,650 on Coinbase, per beaconcha.in data.

Net new validator activations over the past 7 days: 6,240. Net validator exits over the past 7 days: 5,190. Net change: +1,050 validators, per rated.network data for the period July 18–25, 2026.

## Which Validators Are Exiting and What the Wait Time Is

No single labeled entity accounts for a concentration of current exit requests, per beaconcha.in validator data. The exit queue does not identify validator operators by name unless their deposit addresses are publicly labeled.

At the current churn limit of 1,800 exits per day, validators entering the queue on July 25 at 14:00 UTC will complete withdrawal by approximately July 29, 2026. Churn limits adjust based on total active validator count and are set by the protocol, not by any operator.

## What On-Chain Data Confirms

Total ETH staked: 34.1 million ETH, per beaconcha.in as of July 25, 2026. Participation rate: 99.4%. Annualized staking reward rate: 3.2%, per rated.network.
