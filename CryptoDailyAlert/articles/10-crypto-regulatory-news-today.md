# Crypto Regulatory News Today: Enforcement, Licensing, and Rule Changes

**How to use this brief:** This article is an evergreen regulatory news template covering the four primary regulatory bodies active in crypto: SEC (United States), European Securities and Markets Authority / MiCA (EU), FCA (United Kingdom), and CFTC (United States). Sample data uses July 2026 illustrative actions. Replace with live filings when new actions occur.

---

The SEC published a staff bulletin on digital asset custody requirements on July 25, 2026, affecting broker-dealers holding crypto assets on behalf of customers, per the official bulletin at sec.gov/staff-bulletins/2026-digital-asset-custody.pdf.

## What Regulators Announced or Filed on July 25, 2026

**SEC (United States):** The SEC's Division of Trading and Markets published Staff Bulletin 2026-04 on July 25, addressing how broker-dealers must treat customer crypto assets under the Customer Protection Rule (Rule 15c3-3). The bulletin states that broker-dealers may not commingle customer crypto assets with proprietary crypto holdings. The bulletin is not a rule. It is staff guidance, which means it interprets existing rules but does not carry the legal force of a rulemaking. Public comment is not required for staff bulletins.

**EU / MiCA (European Union):** ESMA published a consultation paper on July 22, 2026, covering technical standards for crypto-asset service provider (CASP) registration under MiCA Title V. The consultation period closes on September 15, 2026. Registration requirements under the consultation paper would apply to CASPs operating in any EU member state. "Published a consultation paper" means ESMA is soliciting feedback, not that final rules are in effect.

**FCA (United Kingdom):** The FCA issued a Final Notice on July 21, 2026, to DeltaCoin UK Ltd., withdrawing its registration as a crypto-asset business under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017 (MLRs). The Final Notice is available at fca.org.uk/publication/final-notices/deltacoin-uk-2026.pdf. DeltaCoin UK Ltd. must cease UK crypto-asset business activities with immediate effect, per the Final Notice.

**CFTC (United States):** No new crypto-specific enforcement action or rulemaking was published at cftc.gov between July 21 and July 25, 2026, as of the time of writing.

## Which Entities Are Named or Affected by Each Action

SEC Bulletin 2026-04 applies to all registered broker-dealers holding customer crypto assets under custody. No specific firm is named. The bulletin takes effect as guidance immediately upon publication.

ESMA's MiCA consultation paper affects entities applying for or holding CASP registration across all 27 EU member states. Named categories include crypto-asset exchanges, custody providers, and portfolio management services. Individual firms are not named in the consultation paper.

DeltaCoin UK Ltd. is the sole named entity in the FCA Final Notice. The firm's registration number was FRN 928341. The FCA's register entry for DeltaCoin UK Ltd. was updated to reflect "Cancelled" status on July 21, 2026.

## What the Jurisdictional Scope of Each Action Is

SEC Bulletin 2026-04 applies within the United States to SEC-registered broker-dealers. It does not apply to unregistered crypto exchanges, non-US entities, or entities operating solely as investment advisers.

ESMA's MiCA consultation paper applies to entities operating within the European Union under MiCA's CASP framework. The UK is not subject to MiCA following Brexit. Switzerland is not subject to MiCA.

The FCA Final Notice applies to DeltaCoin UK Ltd.'s operations within the United Kingdom. It does not apply to DeltaCoin entities in other jurisdictions, if any exist. The FCA has not issued statements about non-UK DeltaCoin entities.

## What Remains Pending or Subject to Comment Periods

ESMA's MiCA consultation paper is open for public comment until September 15, 2026. Final technical standards will be published after ESMA reviews responses. The timeline from consultation close to final publication is typically 6-12 months, based on ESMA's prior MiCA rulemaking timelines.

The SEC has not indicated whether Bulletin 2026-04 will be followed by a formal rulemaking on crypto custody. Staff bulletins may precede formal rulemaking or may stand alone indefinitely. No rulemaking notice has been published in the Federal Register in connection with Bulletin 2026-04 as of the time of writing.

DeltaCoin UK Ltd. has the right to refer the FCA's decision to the Upper Tribunal. The deadline to refer is 28 days from the date of the Final Notice, which was July 21, 2026. No referral had been filed as of the time of writing, per the FCA register.

## What to Watch in the Next 30 Days

SEC: Monitor the Federal Register for a proposed rulemaking that may follow Bulletin 2026-04. Formal rulemaking would open a public comment period and carry binding legal force, unlike the current staff bulletin.

EU: The September 15 ESMA consultation deadline is the next milestone. Firms operating as CASPs in the EU should submit responses through the ESMA online consultation tool at esma.europa.eu.

FCA: The August 18 deadline (28 days from July 21) for DeltaCoin UK Ltd. to file a tribunal referral is the next actionable date. If no referral is filed, the cancellation of registration is final.
