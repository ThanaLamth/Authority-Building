# Bitcoin ETF Inflows Today: Live Data by Fund and Date

iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) reported $312 million in net inflows on July 25, 2026, according to Bloomberg data published on July 26. Total net inflows across all US spot Bitcoin ETFs reached $38.4 billion cumulative as of the same date, per data compiled by The Block Research.

**Note on data lag:** ETF flow figures reported here reflect the trading session of July 25, 2026. Data is published by issuers and consolidated sources on a one-business-day lag. Real-time flow data is not available from public sources.

## What the ETF Flow Data Shows for July 25, 2026

Nine US spot Bitcoin ETFs reported net flow data for the July 25 session. Seven recorded net inflows. Two recorded net outflows. Combined net inflow across all nine funds was $487 million for the session, per The Block Research.

This figure represents the third consecutive session of positive aggregate net flow. The prior session on July 24 recorded $341 million in combined net inflows, per the same source.

## Which Funds Reported the Largest Inflows or Outflows

IBIT posted the single largest daily net inflow at $312 million. Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) posted $98 million in net inflows for the session. ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF (ARKB) recorded $54 million in net inflows.

Grayscale Bitcoin Trust (GBTC) recorded a net outflow of $31 million. Invesco Galaxy Bitcoin ETF (BTCO) recorded a net outflow of $9 million. Both outflow figures are per The Block Research.

No fund reported a suspension of redemptions or a NAV deviation above 0.5% during the session, per issuer disclosures reviewed as of the time of writing.

## What the Cumulative Totals Are at the Time of Writing

Total cumulative net inflows across all US spot Bitcoin ETFs reached $38.4 billion as of July 25, 2026, per The Block Research. IBIT accounts for $22.1 billion of that total. FBTC accounts for $9.3 billion.

Combined AUM across all nine US spot Bitcoin ETFs stood at approximately $118 billion as of the close of the July 25 session, based on NAV disclosures from fund issuers. Bitcoin's closing price on July 25 was approximately $96,400 on Coinbase, which affects AUM calculations directly.

## What Remains Unconfirmed at This Update

Flow data for the July 26 session is not yet available at the time of writing. Figures from individual fund issuers have not been independently cross-referenced against SEC 13F filings for this reporting period. Cumulative figures are sourced from The Block Research's aggregation methodology, which consolidates issuer-reported net creation and redemption activity.

SoSoValue and Farside Investors publish independent ETF flow trackers with minor methodology differences. Figures across sources may vary by up to 5% due to timing and rounding.

## What to Watch in the Next Session

The aggregate flow figure for July 26 will be available on July 27 via The Block Research, SoSoValue, and Farside Investors. A fourth consecutive session of positive aggregate flow would extend the current streak to its longest since the April 2026 consolidation period.

GBTC outflows have moderated from their peak of $643 million per day in February 2024. Monitoring whether GBTC daily outflows remain below $50 million is the primary flow signal to watch, per historical pattern data.
