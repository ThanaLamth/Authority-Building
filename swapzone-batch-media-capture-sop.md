# Swapzone Batch — Media Capture SOP
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Applies to:** coinwy/swapzone-batch, Kanalcoin/swapzone-batch, DeFiLiban/swapzone-batch

---

## Global capture rules

- Resolution: 1440px wide browser, 100% zoom
- Format: PNG (screenshots), JPG only for hero images
- Filename: `{article-number}-{platform-slug}-{what}.png`
  - Example: `01-swapzone-homepage.png`, `05-stealthex-btc-xmr-result.png`
- Date in EXIF/filename NOT required — article frontmatter has `last_reviewed`
- Capture LIVE UI — no mockups, no stock photos
- If UI has personal account data visible: blur or crop before saving

## After capture: add image refs to articles

For each captured image, add to the relevant article at the appropriate section:

```markdown
![Swapzone BTC→XMR results showing 6 providers, StealthEX at top rate — July 2026](../media/05-swapzone-btc-xmr-results.png)
*Swapzone query: BTC→XMR, 0.05 BTC, captured July 2026. Provider list and rates will differ at time of reading.*
```

Rules:
- Alt text: describe what the image SHOWS, not what it IS ("Swapzone results showing 6 providers" not "Screenshot of Swapzone")
- Caption: always note the capture date and that rates change
- Place AFTER the comparison table for that article, before the first H3 product section
- For comparison articles (11, 12, 17): place side-by-side images after the main table
