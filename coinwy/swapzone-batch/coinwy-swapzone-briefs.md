## Dàn ý

### H1: Fixed Rate vs Floating Rate Crypto Swap: Which Should You Use?

### Intro (70w)
- Mở: "The problem with floating rates is you send one amount and receive another — sometimes meaningfully less."
- Scope: giải thích cơ chế, không phải product comparison

### H2: How fixed rate works
- Rate locked at time of order creation
- Service provider absorbs slippage risk
- Usually ~0.5–1% premium baked into the quoted rate
- Best for: volatile market conditions, large amounts, predictability needed

### H2: How floating rate works
- Rate confirmed at execution time (after deposit confirmed)
- Market moves between deposit and execution = you receive more or less
- Usually cheaper quoted rate
- Best for: stable pairs, small amounts, speed priority

### Comparison table
| | Fixed Rate | Floating Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Rate lock | Yes | No |
| Slippage risk | None | Yes |
| Quoted rate | Slightly higher | Lower |
| Best market conditions | Volatile | Stable |
| Provider example | Most Swapzone partners | Most Swapzone partners |

### H2: When fixed rate is worth the premium
- BTC amount > 0.1 BTC (≈$10k+)
- Volatile market (>2% daily move)
- Swapping privacy coins (XMR) — execution can take longer

### H2: How Swapzone handles both
- Filter by fixed/float in the interface
- Can see both rate options side by side
- CTA: "See fixed vs floating rates side by side on Swapzone"
