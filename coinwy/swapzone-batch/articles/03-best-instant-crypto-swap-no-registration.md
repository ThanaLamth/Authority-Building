## Fixed Rate vs Floating Rate for Speed

Fixed rate and floating rate affect more than price. They affect behavior at execution.

With a **fixed rate**, the provider locks your quoted receive amount at order creation. If the market moves while the blockchain processes your transaction, the provider absorbs that risk. You get what was quoted. The cost is a premium, typically 0.5-1% above the floating rate.

With a **floating rate**, your receive amount is calculated at execution, not at order creation. If BTC drops 1.5% while your transaction is confirming, you receive 1.5% less. The quoted rate is lower than fixed, but the final amount is not guaranteed.

For speed purposes, fixed rate swaps sometimes take slightly longer because the provider is managing more risk. Floating rate swaps can execute faster on some platforms. The difference is marginal for most pairs.

The practical rule: use fixed rate when swapping volatile pairs, large amounts (above $1,000), or pairs with slow confirmation times like XMR. For stable-to-crypto swaps at small amounts, floating is usually fine. More detail in the [fixed rate vs floating rate breakdown](/exchanges/fixed-rate-vs-floating-rate-crypto-swap).

## FAQ

**What does "no registration" actually mean?**
You do not create a username, password, or profile with the service. You provide only a destination wallet address. Some services (SideShift) ask for an email for swap tracking, which is less than a full account but is not zero data.

**Is "instant" swap actually instant?**
No. "Instant" refers to the swap processing time on the provider's side, which can be seconds to minutes. Actual completion requires blockchain confirmations: 1-3 for ETH (~15-45 sec), 1 for BTC (10+ min), 10-20 for XMR (20-40 min). Fast chains like BNB are fastest.

**Can I swap large amounts without KYC?**
Yes, with caveats. StealthEX has no publicly stated upper limit and no KYC. ChangeNOW applies threshold-triggered KYC at undisclosed amounts. Swapzone routes through providers with different policies, so the no-KYC limit depends on which provider quote you select.

**Which service has the most coins?**
Swapzone as aggregator covers 1,600+ coins across its partner network. ChangeNOW covers 850+, SimpleSwap 900+, StealthEX 700+. SideShift and Exolix have narrower coverage focused on mainstream pairs.

**What if my swap gets stuck?**
Contact the provider's support directly. Swapzone has a support channel that can escalate to the partner. For direct providers, most have a swap ID lookup tool where you can check status and initiate a refund if the swap fails. Funds are returned to your sending address for failed swaps on non-custodial services.

**Is floating rate always cheaper?**
The quoted rate is lower on floating, but the final received amount can be less if the market moves against you during confirmation. For stable pairs or small amounts, floating is usually fine. For volatile pairs or amounts above $1,000, fixed rate often produces a better effective outcome.
