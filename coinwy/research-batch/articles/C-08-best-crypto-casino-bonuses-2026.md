## The EV formula every bonus hunter should know

Before reviewing any bonus category, the formula once more because it changes every decision:

Real Value = Bonus Amount / Wagering Requirement x (1 - House Edge)

The three variables:

Bonus Amount: what the casino gives you. This is the marketed number.
Wagering Requirement (WR): how many times you must bet the bonus before withdrawing. This is often buried in terms.
House Edge: the statistical advantage the casino holds on the games you play with the bonus. Slots typically have 2-5% house edge. Blackjack (if allowed on bonus) has 0.5-1%.

The WR is the key lever the casino controls. A casino can advertise a 500% bonus and set a 100x WR, producing near-zero expected value. A casino with a 50% bonus and 15x WR produces meaningful value.

Slot-restricted bonuses: most casino welcome bonuses can only be wagered on slots. This matters because slot house edge is typically higher than table game house edge. If you prefer blackjack, a bonus that forces slots reduces your EV and your enjoyment.

## Frequently asked questions

What is a good wagering requirement for a crypto casino bonus?
Under 35x is good. 35-45x is standard. Above 50x, the expected value becomes so low that most players will not benefit from the bonus unless they are wagering very high volumes.

What is rakeback and is it better than a welcome bonus?
Rakeback returns a percentage of every wager regardless of win or loss. A welcome bonus is a one-time offer with high wagering requirements. For regular, high-volume players, rakeback produces higher lifetime value than a one-time welcome bonus. For occasional players, a welcome bonus with good terms may be more relevant.

Can I withdraw a no-deposit bonus without depositing?
Usually not directly. No-deposit bonuses typically require meeting a wagering requirement on the free funds, and then require a minimum deposit before withdrawing winnings. The specific rules vary by platform and are detailed in the terms. Read the no-deposit bonus terms before claiming.
