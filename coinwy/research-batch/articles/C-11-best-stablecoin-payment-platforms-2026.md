## The two things that actually matter

Settlement finality and fiat off-ramp. Everything else is secondary.

**Settlement finality** is the time between a transaction being broadcast and it being irreversible. On Solana, this is approximately 400 milliseconds. On Tron, it is approximately 3 seconds. On Ethereum mainnet, it is approximately 12-20 seconds. On Bitcoin, it is 10-60 minutes depending on confirmation count.

For a merchant accepting stablecoin payments, the finality window determines when you can release goods or services. A 400ms Solana USDC payment is effectively instant. A 12-second Ethereum USDT payment requires a brief wait. A Bitcoin payment with 1 confirmation requires 10 minutes minimum.

**Fiat off-ramp quality** determines whether you can actually convert your stablecoin revenue to local currency without excessive friction, fees, or time delay. A stablecoin payment rail that cannot convert to EUR, GBP, or USD within 24 hours at reasonable rates is not a business payment solution; it is a crypto holding position.

### Stripe Crypto

Stripe's stablecoin payment product launched in 2024 and represents the most significant legitimacy signal for crypto payments in B2B markets. The Stripe name carries trust weight that no crypto-native platform has matched.

The product focuses on USDC on Solana: 400ms settlement finality, direct bank off-ramp via Stripe's existing payout infrastructure (ACH for USD, SEPA for EUR). For a merchant already using Stripe for card payments, adding USDC stablecoin acceptance requires minimal integration change.

What stood out from the public interface review was how Stripe frames the product: not as a crypto payment gateway but as a faster, cheaper international payment rail. For merchants processing cross-border B2B invoices where wire transfer fees are $30-50 each, USDC on Solana at near-zero network cost is a concrete cost reduction.

### Coinbase Commerce

Coinbase Commerce allows merchants to accept USDC (native on Base, Coinbase's L2), ETH, BTC, and other assets. Base chain settlement is 400ms. Off-ramp connects directly to Coinbase accounts with standard fiat withdrawal options.

MiCA compliance is Coinbase Commerce's key differentiator for EU merchants: Coinbase holds MiCA CASP licensing through its Irish entity (Coinbase Europe Ltd), which means EU businesses using Coinbase Commerce are working with a MiCA-compliant service provider.

For EU B2B merchants specifically, this compliance status reduces regulatory risk that other platforms in this list cannot provide.

### Transak

Transak is a fiat on/off-ramp aggregator available in 160+ countries. For individual users sending remittances in stablecoins, Transak solves the last-mile problem: converting crypto to local bank accounts in countries where direct crypto-to-bank transfers are uncommon.

The fee range is wide (0.5-5% depending on method and country) because Transak routes through local payment partners whose pricing varies significantly. For popular corridors (EUR, GBP, USD), fees are toward the lower end. For emerging market off-ramps (IDR, NGN, BRL), fees are higher.

What Transak provides that most platforms in this list cannot: 160+ country coverage for fiat off-ramp. For a freelancer in Brazil receiving USDC payments from a US client, Transak's BRL off-ramp is a meaningful service.

Best for: Individual remittance users, wide country coverage for fiat off-ramp, cross-border freelancer payments.
Tradeoffs: Fee range is wide and not fully transparent before transaction. Not a merchant payment gateway. B2B invoice features absent.

### Request Finance

Request Finance is the B2B invoicing tool in this list. It allows businesses to issue invoices in USDT, USDC, or DAI, manage payment records on-chain, and integrate with accounting tools (Xero, QuickBooks).

The product is aimed specifically at crypto-native businesses and DAOs that need to pay contractors, suppliers, or employees in stablecoins. The on-chain invoice record provides an audit trail that spreadsheet-based crypto payments do not.

The weakness: no fiat off-ramp within the platform. Receiving parties convert their stablecoins separately. For businesses whose counterparties need immediate fiat conversion, Request Finance requires a separate off-ramp step.

Best for: Crypto-native businesses, DAOs paying contractors, B2B stablecoin invoicing with on-chain record.
Tradeoffs: No fiat off-ramp integrated. Not for individual use. Receiving party needs crypto wallet.

### Binance Pay

Binance Pay allows merchants to accept USDT, BUSD, and other assets from the 300M+ Binance user base. For merchants whose customers are already Binance users, Binance Pay offers zero-fee crypto-to-crypto transfers within the Binance ecosystem.

The geographic reach is broad: Binance P2P covers fiat off-ramps in IDR, THB, VND, BRL, and many emerging market currencies where other platforms have limited coverage. For merchants in SEA or Latin America, Binance Pay's P2P-connected off-ramp is often the only practical option.

Regulatory caveat: Binance's global regulatory situation has been complex since its DOJ settlement in 2023. Merchants should assess their jurisdiction's regulatory treatment of Binance before integrating.

Best for: Merchants with Binance-using customer base. SEA/LatAm merchants needing local currency off-ramp. Zero-fee crypto-to-crypto.
Tradeoffs: Regulatory complexity around Binance entity. Fiat off-ramp via P2P (not direct bank). BUSD phasing out.

## MiCA compliance status (EU businesses)

MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) requires crypto-asset service providers operating in the EU to hold authorization. For EU businesses choosing a stablecoin payment platform, the MiCA status of their service provider is a compliance consideration.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which stablecoin payment platform is cheapest for large B2B transfers?**
For pure on-chain transfer cost, Solana USDC (via any wallet) has near-zero fees. For a platform-based solution, Binance Pay has zero fees within the Binance ecosystem. For compliance-aware EU businesses, Coinbase Commerce at 1% is the cost of MiCA-compliant processing.

**What is the fastest stablecoin settlement available in 2026?**
Solana (USDC) and Base (USDC) both achieve approximately 400ms finality. Tron (USDT) achieves 3 seconds. Ethereum is 12-20 seconds. For speed-critical applications, Solana and Base are the current leaders.

**Which platform works best for international freelancers getting paid in stablecoins?**
Transak for the widest fiat off-ramp country coverage. Coinbase Commerce if you have a Coinbase account and operate in supported regions. Binance Pay for SEA or LatAm where Binance P2P covers local currency conversion.
