## Is StealthEX a scam?

No. StealthEX is a functioning swap service with 8 years of operating history and one of the higher Trustpilot scores in the non-custodial swap category. The majority of 10,000+ reviews describe successful, fast transactions.

## Specific risks to know

**1. Fake copycat site.** stealth-ex.io (with a hyphen) is fraudulent. Only use stealthex.io.

**2. Large-amount compliance freeze.** Despite published unlimited no-KYC policy, some large transactions are frozen. Rare but documented.

**3. Non-custodial irreversibility.** Errors in destination address are permanent. Double-check before confirming.

**4. Rate discrepancy on floating rate.** Floating rate gives you the market rate at settlement time, not at order creation. Use fixed rate to lock in the displayed amount.

**5. Regulatory jurisdiction.** StealthEX is incorporated in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, a jurisdiction with minimal crypto regulatory oversight. This is common for no-KYC swap services.
