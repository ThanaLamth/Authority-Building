## Key differences

**Coin coverage.** StealthEX lists 1,400+ coins vs SimpleSwap's 1,000+. Both cover privacy coins and major altcoins. For truly obscure tokens, StealthEX has the edge.

**Trustpilot volume.** StealthEX has roughly twice the Trustpilot review count of SimpleSwap (10K vs 5K), both with similar high scores. The higher volume gives slightly more confidence in StealthEX's track record.

**Rate competitiveness.** Both aggregate rates from partner liquidity providers. Rates are generally similar for common pairs. Use an aggregator like Swapzone or Exolix to compare both in real time before committing.

**Fiat support.** Both offer limited fiat via third-party integrations (Simplex, Mercuryo). Neither has the depth of ChangeNOW's 60+ currency card buy program.
