# StealthEX vs ChangeNOW 2026: Which No-KYC Swap Service Should You Use?

*By Coinwy Editorial Team, Reviewed August 2026*

Both StealthEX and ChangeNOW are non-custodial, no-registration crypto swap services with strong Trustpilot track records. The key difference: ChangeNOW will trigger compliance review on large transactions. StealthEX has no published upper KYC threshold and markets genuinely unlimited no-KYC swaps. For standard amounts, ChangeNOW wins on fiat support, volume, and API adoption. For large no-KYC swaps and obscure altcoins, StealthEX is the better documented choice.

## Where StealthEX wins

**No published KYC ceiling.** StealthEX is the documented choice for large no-KYC swaps. ChangeNOW will trigger compliance review at higher amounts. StealthEX has no publicly stated upper limit.

**Coin coverage.** 1,400+ coins vs ChangeNOW's 850+. For obscure altcoins, StealthEX has a meaningful advantage. Privacy coins like XMR and ZEC are consistently available.

**Per-review Trustpilot rating.** StealthEX's 4.8/5 is slightly higher than ChangeNOW's 4.6/5, though the sample is much smaller (10K vs 450K), reducing statistical significance.

---

## Where ChangeNOW wins

**Fiat support.** ChangeNOW integrates fiat card buy across 60+ currencies. StealthEX's fiat options are limited to select pairs via Simplex or Mercuryo. If your starting point is fiat, ChangeNOW is the clearer choice.

**Largest verified track record.** 450,000+ Trustpilot reviews provides a much more statistically significant data set than StealthEX's 10,000+. Both have mostly positive reviews, but the volume difference matters for risk assessment.

**API and wallet integration.** ChangeNOW is integrated into more wallets, dApps, and products than StealthEX. More third-party wallet embedded swap functions are powered by ChangeNOW.

**Settlement on common pairs.** ChangeNOW's documented 5-20 minute range on BTC/ETH/USDT is slightly faster than StealthEX's 5-30 minute range.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is StealthEX better than ChangeNOW?**
It depends on use case. StealthEX wins for large no-KYC swaps and coin coverage. ChangeNOW wins for fiat support and verified track record volume.

**Does StealthEX have a KYC limit?**
No published upper limit. ChangeNOW triggers compliance review on large transactions. Note that some StealthEX users report compliance freezes despite the published policy.

**Can I swap XMR on both?**
Yes. Both services support XMR as of August 2026, though availability can fluctuate based on partner liquidity.
