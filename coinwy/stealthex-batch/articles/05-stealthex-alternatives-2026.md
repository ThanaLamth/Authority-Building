## 1. ChangeNOW, best for fiat and track record

ChangeNOW is the most reviewed non-custodial swap service with 450,000+ Trustpilot reviews at 4.6/5. It supports card buy across 60+ fiat currencies, which StealthEX does not. For standard amounts (no large-transaction KYC risk), ChangeNOW offers a faster documented settlement window (5-20 min vs StealthEX 5-30 min) and wider API adoption. If fiat access is a requirement, ChangeNOW is the primary alternative.

**Best for:** Fiat on-ramp, standard-sized swaps, users who want the largest verified track record.

---

## 2. SimpleSwap, closest direct competitor

SimpleSwap is the closest functional equivalent to StealthEX. Both are non-custodial, registration-free, with 1,000+ coins and fixed/floating rate options. StealthEX has slightly higher Trustpilot volume (10K vs 5K). Rate differences between the two on common pairs are usually under 0.5%, so the choice often comes down to interface preference. Checking both before a large swap is worth the extra minute.

**Best for:** BTC/ETH/USDT swaps when you want a direct StealthEX alternative.

---

## 3. Swapzone, best for rate comparison

Swapzone aggregates quotes from StealthEX, ChangeNOW, SimpleSwap, Exolix, and others, letting you compare rates in real time with no markup. It is not a direct competitor so much as a discovery layer. Use Swapzone first to confirm you are getting the best rate, then complete on whichever provider wins.

**Best for:** Finding the best rate across multiple providers without manual comparisons.

---

## 4. Exolix, for fixed rate focus

Exolix is a smaller direct swap service with a strong fixed-rate focus and no KYC. Its coin coverage (700+) is lower than StealthEX, but it consistently receives positive user feedback. If StealthEX is unavailable for a pair and you want a fixed rate guarantee, Exolix is a clean backup.

**Best for:** Fixed-rate focus, alternative when StealthEX has no rate for a pair.

---

## 5. Trocador, for privacy and Tor

Trocador is an aggregator that supports Tor and does not log user data. It is the privacy-maximalist alternative to StealthEX, surfacing rates from providers who support XMR and other privacy coins. No Trustpilot track record but widely recommended in privacy-focused communities.

**Best for:** Tor users, maximum privacy, no-log swaps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best StealthEX alternative for large swaps?**
ChangeNOW for amounts where KYC is not a concern. For large amounts requiring strict no-KYC, SimpleSwap or Exolix are the closest alternatives, though all have published no-KYC policies that may differ from actual practice at high amounts.

**Which StealthEX alternative has the most coins?**
SimpleSwap (1,000+) is close to StealthEX (1,400+). For very obscure tokens, StealthEX still has the widest single-service coverage.

**Is Swapzone better than StealthEX?**
They solve different problems. Swapzone finds the best rate across providers, including StealthEX. They are complementary, not competing.
