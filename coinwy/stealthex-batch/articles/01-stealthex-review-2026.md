## What StealthEX actually is

StealthEX is a non-custodial swap service. You select the coin you want to send, the coin you want to receive, paste your destination wallet address, and send funds. StealthEX routes through partner liquidity to execute the swap, never holding your funds in custody.

The company emphasizes privacy: no account creation, no email required, no KYC at any publicly stated threshold. This is the core value proposition versus services that impose compliance checks on larger swaps.

StealthEX supports 1,400+ crypto assets, including privacy coins like Monero (XMR) and Zcash (ZEC) that have been delisted from most centralized exchanges.

---

## Rates: fixed vs floating

Like ChangeNOW and SimpleSwap, StealthEX offers two rate modes:

**Fixed rate** locks the exchange rate at the time you create the transaction. Your recipient address receives exactly the displayed amount regardless of price movement during settlement. This costs a small premium.

**Floating rate** gives you the market rate at settlement time. Faster and cheaper but exposes you to slippage if price moves during the 5-30 minute settlement window.

For BTC, ETH, and USDT, the difference is usually 0.2-0.5%. For volatile or low-liquidity altcoins, fixed rate is the safer choice.

## Settlement time

StealthEX documents 5-30 minutes for common pairs. Real user reports generally confirm this range for BTC/ETH pairs. Some privacy coin routes (XMR especially) can run to 40+ minutes depending on block confirmation times.

## KYC policy

StealthEX's published policy is no KYC at any amount. This is distinct from ChangeNOW's approach, which imposes compliance review on large transactions. However, user reports (see Trustpilot section below) indicate that StealthEX has frozen funds in some cases under "compliance review" despite the published policy. For very large amounts, the actual experience may differ from the stated policy.

---

## Fiat support

StealthEX's native service is crypto-to-crypto only. Some pairs include a card buy option via third-party providers (Simplex, Mercuryo), but fiat coverage is significantly narrower than ChangeNOW's 60+ currency card buy integration.
