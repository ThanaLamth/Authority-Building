# StealthEX vs Swapzone 2026: Direct Swap vs Aggregator

*By Coinwy Editorial Team, Reviewed August 2026*

StealthEX and Swapzone solve different problems. StealthEX is a direct swap service, routing your trade through its own liquidity network. Swapzone is an aggregator that pulls rates from multiple providers, including StealthEX itself, and lets you pick the best offer. In many cases, Swapzone will surface a StealthEX rate as one of its top options, so choosing between them is partly a question of whether you want a comparison layer.

| Coins | 1,400+ | Depends on partners |
| Best rate guarantee | StealthEX only | **Compares 10+ providers** |
| Fixed rate | Yes | Yes (per provider) |
| KYC | None stated | Aggregator level (varies by provider) |
| Registration | None | None |
| Trustpilot | 4.8/5 (~10K) | 4.7/5 (~1K) |

## When StealthEX wins

**Speed and simplicity.** One interface, one step. No comparison layer to navigate. For users who trust StealthEX's rates and want to execute immediately, going direct is faster.

**Consistent no-KYC policy.** Swapzone surfaces multiple providers, not all of which have identical KYC policies. On StealthEX directly, the no-KYC commitment applies to the whole transaction.

**Privacy coin availability.** StealthEX consistently lists XMR and ZEC. Aggregators depend on partner availability, which can vary.

---

## When Swapzone wins

**Best rate discovery.** Swapzone pulls quotes from 10+ providers in real time. If StealthEX is offering the best rate on your pair, Swapzone will confirm it. If another provider offers 2-5% better, you will see it.

**No commitment to a single provider.** Different providers handle different pairs better. An aggregator removes the need to manually check StealthEX, ChangeNOW, and SimpleSwap separately.

**No added fee.** Swapzone does not add a fee on top of provider rates. Rate comparisons are accurate.

---

## Recommended workflow

For most users: check Swapzone first for the pair you want. If StealthEX appears as the top or near-top result, complete the swap directly on StealthEX for a cleaner experience. If another provider beats StealthEX by more than 1%, use Swapzone to route through that provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Swapzone include StealthEX?**
Yes. Swapzone aggregates rates from StealthEX and other providers. For many pairs, StealthEX is one of the options shown.

**Is it cheaper to use StealthEX directly or through Swapzone?**
Swapzone does not add a markup, so rates shown are provider rates. Going direct to StealthEX or using Swapzone to confirm the StealthEX rate gives the same result.

**Which is better for XMR swaps?**
StealthEX directly is more reliable for XMR, as partner availability on aggregators fluctuates. Verify current availability on both before initiating.
