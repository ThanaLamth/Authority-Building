# StealthEX No-KYC Guide 2026: How It Works and What to Expect

*By Coinwy Editorial Team, Reviewed August 2026*

StealthEX markets itself as a genuinely no-KYC swap service with no published upper limit. This guide explains what that means in practice, what user reports indicate about actual experience, and how the policy compares to the stated and actual KYC posture of competitors.

---

## What "no KYC" means on StealthEX

StealthEX's published policy:
- No registration required
- No email required
- No identity verification requested for any amount (no stated upper limit)
- Funds are not held in custody, routed directly to your destination wallet

This distinguishes StealthEX from:
- ChangeNOW, which triggers compliance review at higher amounts
- Centralized exchanges (Binance, Kraken), which require full KYC for all users
- Simplex/Mercuryo fiat ramps, which require identity for card purchases

---

## What actually happens in practice

User reports on Trustpilot and Reddit indicate that for standard swap amounts, StealthEX consistently processes transactions without any KYC or identity check. The no-KYC experience is the majority case.

However, a minority of reviews, concentrated on larger amounts, document fund freezes under "compliance review." This contradicts the published unlimited no-KYC policy:

> "My 30 BNB swap has been frozen by StealthEX for over 3 MONTHS under a vague compliance review with zero real resolution."
>
> Tinney Dustin, [* Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a6fafcc64ceb531a8b1f36d), Aug 02, 2026

This pattern is not unique to StealthEX. Most non-custodial swap services that publish unlimited no-KYC policies have some level of AML screening applied to flagged addresses. The published policy reflects the experience for most users, not absolute guarantee for all amounts.

## How to reduce risk on large no-KYC swaps

1. **Use fixed rate.** Floating rate adds slippage risk on top of any compliance risk.
2. **Verify the destination address carefully.** Non-custodial swaps are not reversible. Errors are permanent.
3. **Use a fresh destination address.** For privacy, generate a new receiving address.
4. **Check the StealthEX affiliate/API.** For recurring large volume, the affiliate API program may offer better terms and direct support.
5. **Keep your transaction ID.** If anything freezes, you need the transaction ID to open a support ticket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is StealthEX actually no-KYC?**
Yes for standard amounts. The published policy says no KYC at any amount, and the majority of users experience this. Very large transactions carry a higher risk of compliance review in practice.

**What happens if StealthEX freezes my funds?**
Open a support ticket immediately with your transaction ID. User reports indicate resolution is possible but may take days to months. For disputed amounts, document everything in writing.

**Is there a safer no-KYC alternative for large amounts?**
No published service guarantees unlimited no-KYC with zero exceptions. Trocador (aggregator, Tor-friendly) and Exolix are alternatives with fewer large-amount freeze reports in public review data, but sample sizes are smaller.
