## October betting strategy — structural insights

**UCL group stage:** Group stage matches often involve asymmetric team quality — elite clubs vs promoted qualifiers. Asian handicap on the stronger team can offer better value than the outright result market. Live betting after a goal changes the match state and can offer recalibrated lines.

**NFL:** Player prop markets on NFL are the highest-volume secondary market after game lines. Quarterback passing yards, receiving yards for slot receivers, and touchdown props are the most liquid prop categories.

**UFC:** Method of victory markets (KO/TKO vs submission vs decision) offer better expected value for bettors with fight analysis than straight winner markets where bookmaker models are well-calibrated.
