## Step-by-Step: How to Exchange USDT to BTC on ChangeNOW

### Step 1: Select USDT and choose your network

Go to changenow.io. In the "You send" field, select USDT. A network dropdown will appear: choose between TRC20 (Tron) and ERC20 (Ethereum).

> **Critical: always match the network.** If you select TRC20 in ChangeNOW but send ERC20 USDT to the deposit address (or vice versa), your funds may be permanently unrecoverable. Verify the network shown in ChangeNOW matches the network your wallet is sending from before proceeding. This single error is the leading cause of USDT swap losses on any platform.

TRC20 is preferred for speed: Tron's 3-second block time means your deposit confirms within a minute. ERC20 takes longer (12-15 confirmations required) but may be necessary if your source wallet holds only ERC20 USDT.

### Step 2: Enter your amount and choose a rate type

Enter your USDT amount in the "You send" field. ChangeNOW displays the estimated BTC output in real time. You will see two options: floating rate (settles at market price at execution time) and fixed rate (locks the current rate for a 10-15 minute window).

Select fixed rate for USDT to BTC. BTC price moves continuously; a 1% move during a 10-minute window is plausible. Fixed rate absorbs that variance at a cost of roughly 0.2-0.4% wider spread. For most users, that is a reasonable trade.

### Step 3: Enter your BTC destination address

Paste your Bitcoin wallet receiving address in the "You get" field. Verify the address before confirming, at minimum checking the first and last six characters. BTC transactions are irreversible: sending to an incorrect address results in permanent loss with no recovery path.

### Step 4: Review the swap summary

ChangeNOW displays a complete summary: amount to send, estimated BTC output, network, destination address, rate type, and (for fixed rate) rate expiry time. Review all fields before clicking "Exchange Now."

### Step 5: Send USDT to the deposit address

ChangeNOW generates a unique deposit address valid for your transaction window. Send exactly the specified USDT amount from your wallet to that address on the correct network. Retain your swap ID, displayed on the confirmation screen. You will need it to track progress or contact support.

### Step 6: Monitor and receive

Track your swap at changenow.io/track using your swap ID. Once your deposit confirms on-chain, ChangeNOW begins routing within seconds. BTC arrives at your destination address within the estimated window. TRC20 USDT input to BTC output typically completes in 5-10 minutes under normal Bitcoin mempool conditions.

## Troubleshooting: Common Issues on USDT to BTC Swaps

**Wrong USDT network (the most common critical error)**

Sending TRC20 USDT to an ERC20 deposit address, or ERC20 USDT to a TRC20 address, is the leading cause of fund loss on USDT swaps. If this occurs, contact ChangeNOW support immediately with your swap ID. Recovery depends on whether ChangeNOW's system recognized the deposit and whether the asset is supported. Outcomes vary; recovery is not guaranteed. Prevention is the only reliable safeguard: confirm the network selector in ChangeNOW matches the send network in your wallet before sending.

**Transaction stuck at "Waiting for deposit"**

If your swap shows "Waiting for deposit" more than 20 minutes after sending: first, check the transaction status in a block explorer using the hash from your wallet. If the blockchain has not confirmed the transaction, the delay is on-chain, not with ChangeNOW. TRC20 congestion events are rare. ERC20 under-gas transactions can remain pending for hours in a congested mempool. Once the blockchain confirms the transaction, ChangeNOW typically processes within minutes. If confirmed on-chain but still stuck, contact support with your swap ID and the input transaction hash.

**Received less BTC than quoted on a floating rate swap**

If you selected floating rate and received slightly less BTC than the initial quote, this reflects normal market movement during the execution window. Floating rate settles at market conditions at the time of execution, not at quote time. If the deviation is larger than 1-2% without a corresponding BTC price move during that window, contact support with your swap ID for a review.
