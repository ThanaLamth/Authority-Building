### 1. StealthEX, best overall no-KYC privacy coin service

StealthEX maintains XMR, ZEC, and DASH coverage with no-KYC policy and no registration. Its 1,400+ coin coverage and 8-year track record make it the standard recommendation in privacy-focused communities.

Reddit consensus (r/Monero, r/privacy): StealthEX is routinely recommended alongside Cake Wallet for no-KYC XMR acquisition.

### 2. Trocador, best for maximum privacy

Trocador is an aggregator that works over Tor, accepts no cookies, and logs nothing. It surfaces rates from multiple providers for XMR and other privacy coin pairs. No Trustpilot track record but high trust in privacy communities.

**Best for:** Users who need both on-chain privacy (XMR) and network-layer privacy (Tor).

### 3. ChangeNOW, best for fiat-to-XMR

ChangeNOW maintains XMR support with fiat card buy options. If your entry point is EUR or USD and you want to end up with XMR, ChangeNOW offers the most integrated fiat-to-privacy-coin path. KYC is triggered at threshold on ChangeNOW but the fiat entry requires Simplex-level identity verification regardless.

### 4. Exolix, reliable backup

Exolix maintains XMR support with fixed rate focus and no-KYC policy. Smaller coin selection but reliable for common XMR pairs. Good backup when StealthEX shows no rate for a specific pair.

## Practical privacy considerations

**Swap service is only one part of the privacy stack.**

Using StealthEX over a home connection with your real IP address reduces privacy compared to: StealthEX over VPN + Tor wallet integration (Cake Wallet, for XMR). The coin's on-chain privacy is separate from network-layer privacy.

**Full stack for maximum privacy:**
1. Connect via Tor or VPN
2. Use Trocador (Tor-native aggregator) to discover rates
3. Complete the swap via StealthEX or an aggregated provider
4. Receive XMR into Cake Wallet (full XMR wallet with Tor integration)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where can I swap XMR in 2026?**
StealthEX, ChangeNOW, Trocador, and Exolix all support XMR as of August 2026. Always verify current availability before initiating.

**Is swapping privacy coins legal?**
In most jurisdictions, owning and swapping privacy coins is legal. Tax reporting obligations still apply. Some countries have specific restrictions on XMR. Verify local regulations.

**Why are privacy coins delisted from CEXs?**
Regulatory pressure from FATF travel rule compliance and domestic regulators (FCA in UK, others in EU) has pushed major exchanges to delist XMR and other privacy coins to reduce compliance risk.

**Is StealthEX safe for XMR swaps?**
Yes for standard amounts. Use fixed rate for XMR pairs due to settlement time and volatility. Document your transaction ID in case support is needed.
