## What is RTP and why should you care?

RTP stands for Return to Player. It is a percentage.

The simplest way to think about it: Imagine 1,000 players each spend $100 on the same slot. After millions of spins, the total wagered is $100,000. If the slot has 96 percent RTP, it returns $96,000 in total winnings and keeps $4,000 for the casino.

What this means for you: A slot with 96 percent RTP keeps 4 cents from every dollar you bet on average. A slot with 94.2 percent RTP keeps 5.8 cents. Over 200 spins at $0.10 per spin, that is the difference between losing $0.80 and losing $1.16.

Critical caveat: RTP is an average over millions of spins. In any single session you might win or lose regardless of RTP.

---

## What is volatility?

Volatility describes how wins are distributed over time.

- **Low volatility** = many small wins, few big ones. Your balance moves slowly. Like a steady salary.
- **High volatility** = few wins, but when they hit they can be large. Your balance swings wildly. Like a freelancer with big occasional contracts.
- **Extreme volatility** = you might spin 500 times before a meaningful win. Like a lottery ticket.

If your Halloween session budget is $20, a high-volatility slot can eat through it in 10 minutes with no wins. The same $20 on a low-volatility slot gives you longer play time and more frequent small wins.

---

## What is max win?

Max win is the theoretical maximum prize from a single game round, expressed as a multiplier of your bet.

- 20,000x max win + $1 bet = potential $20,000 payout from one spin
- 20,000x max win + $0.10 bet = potential $2,000 payout

Hitting max win is statistically rare — in some games the frequency is once in tens of millions of spins. Think of it as the slot lottery jackpot.
