## Safety: Avoiding Fake Telegram Casino Bots

This is a serious risk unique to Telegram casinos. Steps to verify you are using the official bot:

1. **Only find bot links from the casino's official website** — never from Telegram groups, forwarded messages, or social media ads
2. **Check the bot's verified badge** — official brand bots on Telegram may have a verified checkmark
3. **Verify the username exactly** — scammers create bots with near-identical names (e.g., @BC_Game_Official vs @BCGame_official_bot)

5. **Deposit only after verifying the bot sends you a deposit address from its own blockchain** — cross-check the deposit address on the casino's web platform if possible
