## Asian Handicap Explained for Vietnamese and Indonesian Players

**Asian handicap (Chấp châu Á / Handicap Asia):** A betting format that eliminates the draw by giving one team a head start or deficit. Compared to European 1X2 (three-way) betting:

- Less likely to lose on a draw
- Usually better odds because margin is split two ways (not three)
- Standard in Vietnamese and Indonesian football betting culture

**Quarter-ball handicap example:** Stake offers -0.25 handicap. This means:
- If favorite wins by any margin: bet wins fully
- If match draws: half-bet wins, half-bet loses (returned)
- If favorite loses: full bet lost

Quarter-ball handicaps reduce the "push" risk and are the most popular format among professional SEA bettors.
