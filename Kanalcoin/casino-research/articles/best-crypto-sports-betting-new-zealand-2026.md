## Legal Framework: Why Offshore Crypto Betting Is Legal in NZ

**Gambling Act 2003, Section 9:** It is unlawful to operate an online gambling service from within New Zealand without a license. The Act does not prohibit New Zealand residents from using offshore gambling services.

**DIA position:** The DIA has consistently stated that it does not pursue individual players using offshore gambling sites. Its enforcement focus is on operators, not consumers.

**Practical result:** Any licensed offshore crypto sportsbook (Curaçao, MGA, Isle of Man) is legally accessible to New Zealand residents. No NZ player has been prosecuted for using offshore gambling sites.

**Tax:** New Zealand has no general capital gains tax. Gambling winnings are generally not taxable for recreational players. Professional gamblers (systematic, profit-oriented) may attract IRD attention — this is rare and fact-specific.
