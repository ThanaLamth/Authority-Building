## Legal Framework: SPA and Crypto in Brazil

**SPA (Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas)** is Brazil's gambling regulator under the Ministry of Finance. Lei 14.790/2023 legalized sports betting and online casino games for licensed operators. Key points:

- SPA issues licenses to operators meeting technical, financial, and responsible gambling standards
- Operators must offer BRL as account currency and accept PIX
- CPF (Brazilian tax ID) is required for licensed operator accounts — KYC is mandatory at SPA-licensed platforms
- Crypto is not prohibited as an additional payment method at SPA-licensed operators
- Offshore crypto casinos (Curaçao-licensed) are accessible to Brazilian players and were the dominant model before January 2025 regulation

**Brazilian player position:** SPA-licensed operators offer full legal protection, mandatory KYC (CPF), PIX deposits. Offshore crypto casinos offer no-KYC access but lack SPA protection. Both are covered in this guide.

**CPF and KYC at crypto casinos:** Curaçao-licensed crypto casinos do not require CPF or Brazilian tax ID — they use their standard KYC thresholds (email only below daily limits). For Brazilian players who prioritize privacy, offshore crypto casinos remain the preferred channel.
