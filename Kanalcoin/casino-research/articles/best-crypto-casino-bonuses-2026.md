## Understanding Wagering Requirements: What They Actually Mean

Wagering requirement (WR) = the multiplier of (bonus amount) or (deposit + bonus) you must bet before withdrawing bonus winnings.

**Example for a Vietnamese player:**
- Deposit: 2,540,000 VND ($100)
- Bonus: 200% = 5,080,000 VND ($200 bonus)
- Wagering: 30x on bonus = 30 × $200 = $6,000 (~152,400,000 VND) must be wagered

**Is 30x reasonable?** Industry standard is 30-45x. Below 30x is generous; above 45x is high.

| WR Level | Industry Classification | Example |
|----------|------------------------|---------|
| 20x or less | Very generous | Rare — check terms carefully |
| 25-30x | Generous | CoinCasino (30x), FortuneJack (30x) |
| 35-40x | Standard | BC.Game (40x), mBit (35x), BetPanda (35x) |
| 45x+ | High | Avoid unless bonus value is exceptional |

**Slot contribution:** Most casinos count 100% of slot bets toward wagering. Live dealer typically contributes 10-20%. If you primarily play live dealer, effective WR is 5-10x higher than stated.

## Bonus Trap Checklist: What to Read Before Claiming

Before claiming any crypto casino bonus, verify:

- [ ] **Game restrictions:** Slots usually qualify 100%. Live dealer often 10-20%. Some bonuses exclude specific titles.
- [ ] **Time limit:** Most bonuses expire in 7-30 days. If you can't complete WR in time, bonus and winnings forfeit.
- [ ] **Maximum bet with bonus active:** Most casinos cap bet size at $5-10 while bonus is active. Exceeding this voids the bonus.
- [ ] **Withdrawal restriction:** With active bonus, withdrawal usually forfeits the bonus. Decide upfront if you want the bonus.
- [ ] **Currency:** BTC bonuses fluctuate with BTC price. USDT bonuses are stable. Know which your bonus is denominated in.
- [ ] **Minimum deposit to trigger bonus:** Most require at least 10-20 USDT. BC.Game's 1 USDT minimum is the exception.
