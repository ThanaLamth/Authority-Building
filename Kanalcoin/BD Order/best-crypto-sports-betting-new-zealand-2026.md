## Legal Framework: Why Offshore Crypto Betting Is Legal in NZ

Gambling Act 2003 Section 9 makes it unlawful to operate an online gambling service from within New Zealand without a license. The Act does not prohibit New Zealand residents from using offshore gambling services.

The DIA (Department of Internal Affairs) has consistently stated it does not pursue individual players using offshore gambling sites. Enforcement focuses on operators. TAB NZ holds the government-issued sports betting license for domestic play.

Tax: New Zealand has no general capital gains tax. Gambling winnings are generally not taxable for recreational players. Professional gamblers operating in a systematic, profit-oriented manner may attract IRD attention -- this is rare and requires specific fact patterns.

The unresolved question: The DIA has been reviewing whether to expand the scope of its gambling legislation to restrict access to offshore platforms at the ISP level. Whether NZ follows the pattern of some EU countries in implementing domain-level blocking for offshore casino sites is not settled as of July 2026. The current legal clarity for NZ players could change if new legislation passes.
