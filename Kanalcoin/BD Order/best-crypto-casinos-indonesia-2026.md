## Legal and OJK Context for Indonesian Players

**What is OJK?** The Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) is Indonesia's integrated financial services authority, regulating banking, capital markets, and non-bank financial institutions. OJK does not regulate offshore crypto casinos, but it does regulate Indonesian banks — which means Indonesian banks are required to block known gambling merchant codes (MCC 7995) in payment processing.

**What this means for crypto casino players:** Direct bank transfers to offshore gambling sites are blocked. Credit cards linked to Indonesian banks will be declined at licensed casino payment gateways. **The crypto casino grey zone in Indonesia:** Playing at an offshore crypto casino is not explicitly criminalized at the individual player level in Indonesian law.
