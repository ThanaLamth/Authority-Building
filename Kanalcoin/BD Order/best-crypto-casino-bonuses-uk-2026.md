## Legal Framework: UKGC Bonus Rules and Crypto

**UKGC bonus advertising rules (2023 update):**
- Bonuses must be clearly labeled with wagering requirements in plain English
- Free spins values must be stated
- Operators cannot offer bonuses that require players to wager more than 10x the bonus + deposit combined (the "10x rule" from UKGC's strengthened guidance)
- No credit card gambling (2020 ban) — debit cards only
- Operators must conduct affordability checks for players showing signs of harm

**UKGC and crypto:** UKGC-licensed operators that accept cryptocurrency are rare — most are debit card and e-wallet focused. The major offshore crypto casinos (BC.Game, Stake, BetPanda, FortuneJack) are not UKGC-licensed and offer bonuses without UKGC's 10x rule restrictions. UK players can access them; enforcement targets operators, not individual players.
