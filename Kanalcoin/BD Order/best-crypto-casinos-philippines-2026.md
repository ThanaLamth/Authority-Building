## Legal Framework: PAGCOR and Crypto Casinos

**PAGCOR (Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation)** is the government-owned-and-controlled corporation that regulates all gambling in the Philippines under Presidential Decree 1869. PAGCOR issues two relevant license types for online operators:

- **PIGO** (Philippine Inland Gaming Operator) — for operators serving Filipino residents domestically
- **POGO** (Philippine Offshore Gaming Operator) — for operators serving players outside the Philippines

As of 2026, PAGCOR has tightened POGO regulation significantly, while PIGO licenses remain the path for operators serving Filipino residents. Crypto casino platforms operating under Curaçao or MGA licenses are accessible to Filipino players; these operators are not locally licensed but are not prohibited at the player level.

**Philippine player position:** Playing at a PAGCOR-licensed operator is fully legal. Playing at a Curaçao-licensed offshore crypto casino is in a grey zone that Filipino authorities have not enforced against individual players. This guide covers both categories.
