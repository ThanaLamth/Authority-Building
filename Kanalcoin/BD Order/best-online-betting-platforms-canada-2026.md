## Legal Framework: iGaming Ontario and Crypto

The Canada Revenue Agency considers gambling income taxable only if it is a primary income source or conducted in a systematic, professional manner.

Several iGO-registered operators have added cryptocurrency deposit options in 2025-2026. However, full AML and KYC requirements apply -- SIN (Social Insurance Number) is required for large transactions. Curacao-licensed crypto casinos (BC.Game, BetPanda, Stake, CoinCasino) are accessible to Ontario players and operate in a grey zone -- not iGO-registered, lacking player fund guarantees.

Outside Ontario: BC, Manitoba, and Quebec have government-operated gambling monopolies. Alberta and other provinces have no specific licensed private operator framework. No Canadian province has prosecuted individual players for offshore online gambling.

The unresolved question: AGCO is reviewing whether additional consumer protection measures -- including potential mandatory identification of crypto on-ramps feeding iGO-adjacent platforms -- will apply to unlicensed crypto casinos accessible to Ontario players. The regulatory boundary between iGO and offshore crypto platforms may narrow in 2026-2027.
