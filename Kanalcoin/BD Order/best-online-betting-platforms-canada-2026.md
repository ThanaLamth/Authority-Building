## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is online gambling legal in Canada?**
In Ontario, iGaming Ontario-registered operators are fully legal. Other provinces: BC, Manitoba, and Quebec have government-operated sites (legal). Private online casino play outside government sites is in a grey zone — no Canadian province has prosecuted individual players. Federal Criminal Code Section 207 governs gambling; provincial licensing determines legality of individual platforms.

**Are crypto casino winnings taxable in Canada?**
Generally, gambling winnings are not taxable in Canada for casual players (not considered a source of income). The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) considers gambling income taxable only if it is your primary income source or you play in a professional, systematic manner. Occasional recreational play at crypto casinos is generally not taxed. Consult a Canadian tax advisor for your specific situation.

**How do I deposit at a crypto casino from Canada without triggering AML review?**

Keeping P2P transactions below this level individually avoids automatic reporting triggers.
