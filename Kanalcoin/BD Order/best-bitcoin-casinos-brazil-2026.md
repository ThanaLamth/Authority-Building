## Legal Framework: SPA and Crypto in Brazil

SPA (Secretaria de Premios e Apostas) is Brazil's gambling regulator under the Ministry of Finance. Lei 14.790/2023 legalized sports betting and online casino games for licensed operators. Operators must offer BRL as account currency, accept PIX, and require CPF (Brazilian tax ID) for account registration. Crypto is not prohibited as an additional payment method at SPA-licensed platforms.

Offshore crypto casinos (Curacao-licensed) were the dominant model before January 2025. They are accessible to Brazilian players without restriction. They do not require CPF. BetPanda via Web3 wallet login requires no personal data at all.

Brazilian player position: SPA-licensed operators offer full legal protection and PIX direct. Offshore crypto casinos offer no-KYC access but lack SPA consumer protections. Both categories are covered in this guide.

The unresolved question: SPA has not finalized its position on cryptocurrency as a deposit method for licensed operators. Whether SPA will mandate BRL conversion requirements for crypto deposits at licensed platforms -- or restrict offshore crypto casino access at the ISP level -- is not yet settled as of July 2026. Players using offshore platforms should monitor SPA announcements at apostas.fazenda.gov.br.
