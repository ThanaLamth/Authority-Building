## 1. StealthEX, best for large no-KYC and privacy coins

StealthEX has operated since 2018 and maintains the clearest public no-KYC-at-any-amount policy among major swap services. Its 1,400+ coin coverage is the widest in the direct service category. For XMR and other privacy coins that have been delisted from centralized exchanges, StealthEX is one of the most consistent options.

**Key stats:** 4.8/5 Trustpilot, 10K+ reviews, 1,400+ coins, no registration, fixed and floating rate.

**Limitation:** Compliance freezes documented on some large transactions despite published unlimited no-KYC policy.

## 2. ChangeNOW, best for fiat and volume

ChangeNOW has the largest Trustpilot sample in the non-custodial swap category (450,000+ reviews). It supports card buy across 60+ fiat currencies. For users who need fiat-to-crypto without full CEX KYC, ChangeNOW is the better-integrated option. KYC is triggered at threshold for large swaps.

**Key stats:** 4.6/5 Trustpilot, 450K+ reviews, 850+ coins, 60+ fiat currencies.

## 4. Exolix, for fixed rate focus

Exolix emphasizes its fixed rate guarantee and no-KYC policy. Smaller coin selection (700+) but strong user reviews relative to review volume. Clean interface and reliable settlement on common pairs.

## 5. Trocador, for maximum privacy

Trocador is an aggregator that supports Tor browsing, accepts no cookies, and logs nothing. It aggregates providers that support privacy coins. No Trustpilot track record, but widely cited in Monero and privacy communities. For users where the network connection itself must be private, Trocador is the standard recommendation.

## What no-KYC actually means in 2026

Even services that publish unlimited no-KYC policies apply some level of AML screening to flagged addresses. "No KYC" means no identity document collection, not no AML. For the vast majority of users with clean wallet history, no-KYC swap services process transactions without any identity interaction. Large amounts and addresses flagged by chain analysis tools are the exception.
