# StealthEX Fiat to Crypto 2026: Buying Crypto with a Card

*By Kanalcoin Research Team, Reviewed August 2026*

StealthEX is fundamentally a crypto-to-crypto swap service. Fiat support is available but limited, powered by third-party payment processors (Simplex and Mercuryo) rather than StealthEX's own infrastructure. This guide explains how it works, what is and is not available, and when ChangeNOW is the better choice for fiat purchases.

---

## How StealthEX fiat buy works

When you create a swap on StealthEX and select a coin pair that includes a fiat entry, a "Buy with card" button may appear. Clicking it routes you to either Simplex or Mercuryo, depending on your country and currency.

These are separate businesses from StealthEX:
- **Simplex** is a licensed payment processor. They apply their own KYC and fraud screening.
- **Mercuryo** is a crypto payment gateway with similar KYC requirements.

StealthEX itself does not handle your card data or payment. The swap to crypto happens after the fiat purchase completes on the third-party side.

---

## What currencies are supported

Simplex and Mercuryo support varies by country. Generally available:
- USD, EUR, GBP
- Select other currencies depending on processor availability

Not all card types are accepted. Debit cards generally have higher acceptance rates than credit cards on Simplex for crypto purchases in some regions.
