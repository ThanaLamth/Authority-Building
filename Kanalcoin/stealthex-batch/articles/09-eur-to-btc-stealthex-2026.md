# EUR to BTC via StealthEX 2026: How to Buy Bitcoin with Euros

*By Kanalcoin Research Team, Reviewed August 2026*

> **Important note on fiat**
>
> StealthEX is primarily a crypto-to-crypto swap service. Direct EUR-to-BTC via card on StealthEX is available on select pairs through third-party integrations (Simplex, Mercuryo), but is not StealthEX's native strength. This guide covers both the StealthEX fiat option and the recommended two-step alternative for users who need no-KYC EUR-to-BTC.

---

## The EUR to BTC challenge in 2026

Buying Bitcoin with euros without KYC is harder than it used to be. Most major options now require identity verification:

- **Binance/Kraken:** Full KYC required for EUR deposits
- **Coinbase:** Full KYC required
- **Revolut/PayPal crypto:** Custodial only, full KYC

The non-KYC paths that remain:

1. **P2P platforms (Bisq, HodlHodl):** True no-KYC but slower and less liquid
2. **Bitcoin ATMs:** No-KYC up to certain limits, high fees
3. **StealthEX card buy via Simplex:** Available on some pairs, KYC applied by Simplex
4. **Two-step: EUR-to-stablecoin on a lenient platform, then swap to BTC via StealthEX**

---

## StealthEX EUR to BTC: what is actually available

StealthEX's native service is crypto-to-crypto. For EUR-to-BTC, some pairs offer a card purchase option powered by Simplex or Mercuryo, displayed during the swap flow. These providers apply their own KYC requirements.

If Simplex integration is available for your currency and card, you will see a "Buy with card" option during the StealthEX swap creation. Acceptance depends on Simplex's country and card approval, not StealthEX itself.

**StealthEX card buy coverage:** Select EUR cards accepted, varies by issuing bank and country. A Reddit user reported: "stealthex.io has worked for me multiple times with just my credit card but I cannot say if it will go through with your specific UK bank."

## Recommended two-step EUR to BTC (truly no-KYC)

For users who cannot get card approval via Simplex on StealthEX, or who want to avoid any identity check:

**Step 1:** Convert EUR to a stablecoin (USDT or USDC) using a service with minimal KYC requirements (some P2P platforms, Bitcoin ATMs with low limits, or lenient card providers).

**Step 2:** Swap the stablecoin to BTC on StealthEX (crypto-to-crypto, no KYC, no registration).

This two-step route gives you BTC without creating a full identity trail, as the StealthEX step is anonymous.

---

## Rate guide for EUR to BTC

EUR/BTC rates depend on:
- EUR-to-USDT spread (if using two-step)
- USDT-to-BTC rate on StealthEX (typically within 0.3-0.8% of market)
- StealthEX fixed vs floating rate choice
- Payment processing fee from Simplex/Mercuryo (if using card buy)

For the total cost on a two-step route, budget 1-3% above spot depending on entry method and StealthEX rate mode.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I buy BTC with EUR on StealthEX without KYC?**
The native StealthEX crypto-to-crypto swap requires no KYC. The card buy option (via Simplex) has Simplex's own KYC process. True no-KYC EUR to BTC is better achieved via the two-step method.

**What is the StealthEX EUR to BTC rate?**
StealthEX rates on USDT/BTC pairs are generally within 0.3-0.8% of market rate on floating mode, or slightly higher with fixed rate. Card purchase via Simplex adds a 3-5% processing fee.

**Does StealthEX accept Revolut for EUR purchases?**
Acceptance depends on Simplex's payment processing, not StealthEX. Revolut cards have variable acceptance on Simplex depending on your account type and country.
