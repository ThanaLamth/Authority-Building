## Step-by-Step: How to Swap BTC to ETH on ChangeNOW

This is the complete walkthrough for executing a BTC to ETH swap on ChangeNOW, with the common mistakes called out at each stage.

In the "You Send" field, select BTC. In the "You Receive" field, select ETH. The live rate appears immediately. With 1000+ assets on ChangeNOW, use the search box to confirm ETH on Ethereum mainnet — not an ETH-named token on another chain.

**Step 3: Enter your BTC amount and choose rate type.**
Enter the BTC amount you want to send. The global minimum is $2 equivalent; the interface displays the current minimum in BTC terms dynamically. As of October 2026, BTC minimums typically sit around 0.0005 to 0.001 BTC. There is no published upper limit. You will see two rate options: Floating and Fixed. The estimated ETH output is shown for both modes. Choose based on your precision needs.

**Step 4: Enter your ETH receiving wallet address.**
This is the most critical step. Enter a standard Ethereum mainnet address (starts with 0x). Accuracy is absolute — crypto transactions are irreversible, and ETH sent to a wrong address cannot be recovered. Verify:
- The address is an Ethereum mainnet (ERC-20) address, not a BSC (BEP-20) address (both start with 0x, which causes confusion)
- You are not using an address configured for a different blockchain
- If sending to an exchange deposit, confirm the exchange supports incoming ETH to that address

**Step 5: Send the exact BTC amount to the deposit address.**
After clicking Exchange, ChangeNOW generates a unique Bitcoin deposit address. Send exactly the BTC amount shown — not more, not less. Under-sending may result in the swap processing at a reduced rate or requiring manual support. Over-sending is not automatically refunded at identical rate. Use your wallet's send function to copy-paste the deposit address rather than retyping it.

**Step 6: Track via block explorer and ChangeNOW's status page.**
ChangeNOW provides a live transaction status page. You can also track your BTC deposit on a Bitcoin block explorer. ChangeNOW typically waits for 1 to 3 BTC confirmations before processing the swap — each Bitcoin block takes approximately 10 minutes, so the first confirmation alone can take 5 to 15 minutes depending on network conditions.

**Step 7: Receive ETH in your wallet.**
Once ChangeNOW processes the swap, ETH is broadcast to your address. Total end-to-end time is typically 15 to 25 minutes including BTC confirmation wait time. ETH settles within one or two Ethereum blocks after ChangeNOW broadcasts.

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- **BEP-20 vs ERC-20 confusion.** Both use 0x addresses. If you paste a BNB Chain address, ETH will arrive on BNB Chain, not Ethereum mainnet. Verify the network in your wallet settings before copying the address.
- **Sending from a slow exchange.** If you send BTC from an exchange that batches withdrawals, the 15 to 25 minute estimate does not start until the exchange actually broadcasts your BTC transaction. Add 30 to 60 minutes buffer if withdrawing from a major CEX.
- **Fixed rate expiry.** Fixed rates have a validity window, usually around 20 minutes. If you select Fixed rate but take too long to send BTC, the rate lapses to floating. Send immediately after selecting Fixed.
- **Incorrect deposit amount.** ChangeNOW generates a deposit address for your specific quoted amount. Do not split the send across multiple transactions unless ChangeNOW support has specifically instructed you to.

## The ERC-20 Address Note: Avoid a Common Mistake

When receiving ETH, your destination address must be a standard Ethereum mainnet address (beginning with 0x). A common mistake is sending to:

- **A Coinbase or Binance exchange deposit address.** Technically valid for ETH, but you are depositing to a custodial account rather than a self-custody wallet. If the exchange does not recognise the incoming ETH transaction format or if your account is restricted, this can create complications. Prefer a self-custody wallet (MetaMask, Ledger, hardware wallet) when possible.
- **A BEP-20 (Binance Smart Chain) address.** BEP-20 addresses have the same 0x format as Ethereum, which is a frequent source of confusion. If you send ETH to a BEP-20 address, funds end up on BSC, not Ethereum mainnet. Verify the network in your wallet settings before copying the address.

## Gas Fees on the Receiving End

When ETH arrives in your wallet, you do not pay gas to receive it — the recipient of ETH does not pay gas. ChangeNOW handles the BTC-to-ETH conversion and bears the gas cost of broadcasting the ETH transaction, which is factored into their service spread.

However, if you want to do anything with the received ETH after it arrives — send it, stake it, use it in DeFi — you will need ETH for gas at that point. ChangeNOW's service ends at delivery. Plan your subsequent ETH usage accordingly.
