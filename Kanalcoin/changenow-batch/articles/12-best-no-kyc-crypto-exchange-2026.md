## What "No KYC" Actually Means in 2026

No-KYC platforms are not regulation-free. All five platforms here operate AML screening, they check deposit addresses against sanctioned wallet databases and may pause large or flagged transfers for manual review. The distinction is that no upfront identity verification is required for standard swaps. You do not create an account. You do not submit a passport scan. You enter a destination address and send funds.

Compliance holds are threshold-triggered, not routine. For the overwhelming majority of users, those transacting in normal volumes with legitimately sourced funds, the no-KYC experience is end-to-end.

## Troubleshooting Common No-KYC Exchange Issues

**Transaction stuck / not arriving:**
Check the swap tracker using your swap ID on the platform you used. For ChangeNOW: changenow.io tracks all swaps by ID. If the status shows "Confirming," wait for the required network confirmations (BTC: 1–3, XMR: 10, ETH: 12). If stuck beyond 60 minutes, contact support via live chat with your swap ID. Average ChangeNOW first response is under 15 minutes via live chat.

**Wrong network sent:**
Contact the platform's support immediately with your swap ID and the transaction hash. ChangeNOW and StealthEX both have policies to attempt recovery if the asset is supported on the erroneously used network. Recovery is not guaranteed but has been resolved in documented cases. Act quickly — delays reduce recovery options.

**Rate changed from what was quoted:**
Floating rate swaps execute at market price at the time of settlement, which may differ from the initial quote. For price certainty, always select Fixed Rate at initiation. Fixed Rate locks the exchange rate regardless of market movement during the deposit window, at the cost of a slightly less favourable base rate.

**Support not responding:**
ChangeNOW operates 24/7 live chat — the most responsive support of the five platforms listed here. StealthEX also has live chat. Trocador, Exolix, and Godex support is primarily ticket-based with variable response times. For urgent issues, ChangeNOW's support infrastructure is a key differentiator.

**No-KYC exchange vs DEX: which is safer?**
No-KYC centralised swap services (ChangeNOW, StealthEX) offer customer support, rate guarantees, and recovery assistance for stuck transactions. DEXes (Uniswap, THORChain) are fully non-custodial and cannot be shut down, but offer no support for user errors and have more complex interfaces. For most retail users, a no-KYC centralised swap service is safer in practice because human support is available when something goes wrong.

**Can authorities track no-KYC swaps?**
Blockchain transactions are publicly visible on transparent chains (BTC, ETH). A no-KYC exchange does not link your swap to your identity, but the on-chain flow is traceable if your sending or receiving address is already associated with your identity. Privacy coins like XMR obscure the on-chain trail. For most users doing ordinary crypto conversions, the practical privacy of a no-KYC swap is meaningful; for users with specific operational security requirements, additional measures (such as using Monero as an intermediate) may be appropriate.
