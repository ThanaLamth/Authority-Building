## Fixed Rate vs Floating Rate: Which Should You Choose?

ChangeNOW offers two rate modes for ETH to BTC:

- **Floating rate.** The rate at execution is determined by market conditions at the moment your deposit is confirmed. You may receive slightly more or slightly less BTC than quoted. Best when markets are stable or you are comfortable with small variance.
- **Fixed rate.** ChangeNOW locks your rate at initiation. You know exactly how much BTC you will receive before sending ETH. The fixed rate typically carries a small premium (the spread is slightly wider), but it eliminates price-change risk during the 5 to 20 minute settlement window. Best when you are swapping a specific amount for a specific purpose.

For most users making a routine ETH to BTC conversion, the floating rate is fine and usually delivers a marginally better outcome. If you are swapping to match an exact BTC amount for a payment or purchase, the fixed rate is worth the slight premium. Given ETH's 6.84% decline over the past seven days, price volatility is elevated — the fixed rate is a particularly sensible choice in these conditions.

## Step-by-Step: How to Swap ETH to BTC on ChangeNOW

This is the complete walkthrough for executing an ETH to BTC swap on ChangeNOW, including the common mistakes that cause delays or lost funds.

**Step 1: Navigate to changenow.io.**
Go directly to changenow.io in your browser. Confirm you are on the main Exchange tab, not the Buy tab (which is for fiat-to-crypto purchases). Bookmark the correct URL to avoid phishing sites.

**Step 2: Select the ETH to BTC pair.**
In the "You Send" field, select ETH. In the "You Receive" field, select BTC. The interface will load the live rate immediately. ChangeNOW supports 1000+ assets, so use the search field to find the exact coin if needed.

**Step 3: Enter your ETH amount and choose rate type.**
Type the ETH amount you want to send. The minimum is approximately $2 equivalent (displayed dynamically in the interface as of the current market price). There is no published upper limit. Below the amount field, you will see two rate options: Floating and Fixed. The interface shows the estimated BTC output for both. Choose Fixed if you need certainty; choose Floating if you want to optimise for rate.

**Step 4: Enter your BTC receiving address.**
This is the most critical step. Enter the Bitcoin wallet address where you want to receive BTC. Accuracy here is non-negotiable — crypto transactions are irreversible, and BTC sent to a wrong address is unrecoverable. Double-check:
- The address is a valid Bitcoin address (starts with 1, 3, or bc1)
- You are not using an Ethereum or altcoin address by mistake
- If sending to an exchange deposit address, confirm the exchange accepts external BTC deposits to that address

**Step 5: Confirm and send your ETH deposit.**
After clicking Exchange, ChangeNOW generates a unique ETH deposit address for your transaction. Send the exact ETH amount specified — sending more or less than the quoted amount can cause the swap to be processed at a revised rate or require manual intervention. Your ETH transaction must include sufficient gas to confirm on Ethereum mainnet.

**Step 6: Track your transaction.**
ChangeNOW provides a transaction tracking page with a status indicator. You can also monitor your ETH deposit on Etherscan. Once the deposit confirms (typically 1 to 3 Ethereum blocks), ChangeNOW processes the swap and broadcasts the BTC transaction. Track the BTC output on a Bitcoin block explorer using the transaction ID ChangeNOW provides.

**Step 7: Receive BTC.**
BTC arrives in your wallet within 5 to 20 minutes of ETH deposit confirmation for most network conditions. During high Ethereum gas periods, the deposit confirmation itself may extend the timeline.

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- **Wrong network on receiving address.** A BTC address is not an ETH address. Confirm the receive field is set to BTC.
- **Sending from an exchange with slow withdrawal.** If you initiate the ETH send from an exchange that batches withdrawals, the 5 to 20 minute estimate begins only after the ETH actually hits ChangeNOW's deposit address — not when you clicked "withdraw" on your exchange.
- **Fixed rate expiry.** Fixed rates have a time window (typically 20 minutes from initiation). If you do not send your ETH deposit before the fixed rate expires, you will be switched to the floating rate. Initiate the ETH send immediately after selecting Fixed.
- **Underpaying gas on the ETH send.** A low-gas ETH transaction may take hours to confirm. Set gas to at least the recommended standard level, especially during high-congestion periods.

## Security and Privacy: What ETH to BTC Users Should Know

ChangeNOW is non-custodial by design: it never holds your funds for longer than the duration of an active swap. Your ETH is forwarded to a liquidity provider and BTC is sourced and sent to your address. There is no ChangeNOW balance or account that holds your assets.

For ETH to BTC specifically, this is a low-compliance-risk pair. Both ETH and BTC are mainstream assets with deep liquidity and no delisting pressure from major platforms. There is no regulatory sensitivity associated with this pair that would elevate your compliance exposure above a standard crypto transaction.

Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction. ChangeNOW does not issue tax documents, so you are responsible for tracking swap amounts, dates, and BTC received. Consult a local tax professional for jurisdiction-specific guidance.
